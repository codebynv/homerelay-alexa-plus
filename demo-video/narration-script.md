### Segment 1: Hook (0:00 – 0:10 | 10.0s)
> "Managing a household means juggling meals, groceries, errands, and reminders. HomeRelay brings these tasks together in one coordinated, intelligent workflow."

### Segment 2: Problem & Solution (0:10 – 0:22 | 12.4s)
> "Instead of scattered apps and unverified AI guesses, HomeRelay provides a single unified dashboard connecting your live pantry, family members, and smart home tools with verified, deterministic planning."

### Segment 3: Pantry & Meal Planning (0:22 – 0:39 | 16.7s)
> "Here in the pantry inventory, we track available staples in real time. When you need a meal plan, simply enter a natural goal, like 'Plan dinner for 4 guests under $50'. HomeRelay instantly parses the request, checks recipe requirements against live ingredients, and computes what is needed."

### Segment 4: Actionable Plan & Human-in-the-Loop (0:39 – 0:52 | 13.5s)
> "Crucially, HomeRelay never mutates state without your permission. It constructs a multi-step Execution Plan showing pantry verification, shopping requirements, and dinner reminders. You review the proposed actions before confirming."

### Segment 5: Execution, Audit Trail & Alexa+ (0:52 – 1:10 | 17.2s)
> "Once confirmed, the actions execute deterministically and log to an immutable audit trail. Every tool is exposed via an official Model Context Protocol Streamable HTTP endpoint, allowing Alexa voice skills to invoke pantry checks and coordination seamlessly."

### Segment 6: Closing (1:10 – 1:28 | 14.8s)
> "HomeRelay turns everyday household requests into organized, reviewable plans. One place to plan, coordinate, and stay on top of home life. Built with React, Node.js, AWS SAM, and the Model Context Protocol."
